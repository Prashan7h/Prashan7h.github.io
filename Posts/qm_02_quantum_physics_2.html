8.05 Quantum Physics II — Course Summary

Zwiebach, Mastering Quantum Mechanics Part II (Ch 12–23) + MIT 8.05 notes (2013)

The course in 8 ideas

  1. New language, same physics: a state is a vector $|\Psi\rangle$ in a complex Hilbert space; the wavefunction is its components, $\psi(x)=\langle x|\Psi\rangle$.
  2. It's all linear algebra: Hermitian ⇒ real eigenvalues + orthonormal eigenbasis (spectral theorem). Measurement = projection onto an eigenspace.
  3. Compatible observables commute ⇔ share an eigenbasis; a CSCO labels states uniquely. Non-commuting pairs obey $\Delta A\Delta B\ge|\langle[A,B]\rangle|/2$.
  4. Time evolution is unitary, $\mathcal U=e^{-i\hat Ht/\hbar}$. Schrödinger (states move) and Heisenberg (operators move) pictures give identical predictions.
  5. Spin ½ is the simplest quantum system. Any two-level system is a spin in an effective field and precesses at $\omega=2|\vec g|/\hbar$.
  6. Oscillator beyond $|n\rangle$: coherent states move like classical particles with minimal noise; squeezed states trade $\Delta x$ for $\Delta p$.
  7. Composite systems = tensor products. Entanglement powers teleportation and Bell violations; subsystems are described by density matrices; decoherence is entanglement with the environment.
  8. Angular momentum from commutators alone: $j=0,\frac12,1,\dots$; ladder operators; $j_1\otimes j_2=\bigoplus j$ explains hyperfine and spin-orbit structure.

1 · Wave mechanics refresher notes 1 · Ch 6–7

$$i\hbar\,\partial_t\Psi=\Big(-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2+V\Big)\Psi,\qquad \Psi=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$$
  • 1D bound states: non-degenerate, can be chosen real; even $V$ ⇒ bound states even/odd; $n$-th state has $n-1$ nodes; $E>V_{\min}$.
  • Spectrum: $E<\min(V_{-\infty},V_{+\infty})$: discrete, non-degenerate. Between the two asymptotes: continuous, non-degenerate. Above both: continuous, doubly degenerate.
  • Matching: $\psi$ always continuous; $\psi'$ continuous unless δ-function or infinite wall. $V=-\alpha\delta(x)$: $\Delta\psi'(0)=-\frac{2m\alpha}{\hbar^2}\psi(0)$.
Variational principle. For any normalizable $\psi$: $$E_{\rm gs}\le\frac{\langle\psi|\hat H|\psi\rangle}{\langle\psi|\psi\rangle}=\frac{\int\big(\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}|\psi'|^2+V|\psi|^2\big)dx}{\int|\psi|^2dx}$$ Equality iff $\psi$ is the ground state. Error in $E$ is second order in the error in $\psi$. Use trial functions with parameters, minimize. Any attractive 1D potential has a bound state.

Position & momentum: $\hat p=\frac\hbar i\partial_x$, $\tilde\psi(p)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\int e^{-ipx/\hbar}\psi(x)dx$; in $p$-space $\hat x=i\hbar\,\partial_p$.

2 · Spin one-half notes 2 · Ch 12, 14.7

Stern–Gerlach: $\vec F=\nabla(\vec\mu\cdot\vec B)$ splits silver atoms into 2 beams ⇒ two-state system. Sequential SG boxes: $z$-filter then $x$-filter gives ½/½; states $|z;+\rangle,|z;-\rangle$ orthogonal (not "opposite vectors").

$$\hat{\vec S}=\frac\hbar2\vec\sigma,\quad \sigma_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\ \sigma_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\ \sigma_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$$ $$[\hat S_i,\hat S_j]=i\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}\hat S_k,\qquad \hat S^2=\tfrac34\hbar^2\,\mathbb 1$$ $$\sigma_i\sigma_j=\delta_{ij}\mathbb 1+i\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k,\quad [\sigma_i,\sigma_j]=2i\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k,\quad\{\sigma_i,\sigma_j\}=2\delta_{ij}$$ $$(\vec a\cdot\vec\sigma)(\vec b\cdot\vec\sigma)=(\vec a\cdot\vec b)\,\mathbb 1+i(\vec a\times\vec b)\cdot\vec\sigma$$ $$e^{i\theta\,\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma}=\cos\theta\,\mathbb 1+i\sin\theta\,\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma\quad(|\hat n|=1)$$

$\sigma_i^2=\mathbb 1$, $\text{tr}\,\sigma_i=0$, $\det\sigma_i=-1$, eigenvalues $\pm1$, $\text{tr}(\sigma_i\sigma_j)=2\delta_{ij}$. Any $2\times2$: $M=a_0\mathbb1+\vec a\cdot\vec\sigma$, $a_0=\frac12\text{tr}M$, $a_i=\frac12\text{tr}(M\sigma_i)$.

Spin along $\hat n=(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi,\cos\theta)$: $\hat S_n=\hat n\cdot\hat{\vec S}$ $$|n;+\rangle=\cos\tfrac\theta2|+\rangle+\sin\tfrac\theta2\,e^{i\phi}|-\rangle$$ $$|n;-\rangle=\sin\tfrac\theta2|+\rangle-\cos\tfrac\theta2\,e^{i\phi}|-\rangle$$ $$|x;\pm\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(|+\rangle\pm|-\rangle),\qquad |y;\pm\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(|+\rangle\pm i|-\rangle)$$ $\langle n;+|\hat{\vec S}|n;+\rangle=\frac\hbar2\hat n$; $|\langle n';+|n;+\rangle|^2=\cos^2\frac{\gamma}{2}$ ($\gamma$ = angle between $\hat n,\hat n'$).

Every spin-½ state is $|n;+\rangle$ for some $\hat n$ (Bloch sphere). Orthogonal states ↔ antipodal points.

Rotating spin states: $\hat R_{\hat n}(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha\hat n\cdot\hat{\vec S}/\hbar}=\cos\frac\alpha2\,\mathbb1-i\sin\frac\alpha2\,\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma$ rotates $\langle\hat{\vec S}\rangle$ by α about $\hat n$; $\hat R(2\pi)=-\mathbb1$ (spinors need 4π).
Quantum key distribution (BB84). Alice sends random bits, each encoded in a random basis ($z$ or $x$). Bob measures in a random basis. They publicly compare bases (not bits) and keep the ~½ that match: the sifted key. An eavesdropper who intercepts and resends in a random basis causes 25% errors in the sifted key, so comparing a sample reveals her. Security rests on no-cloning and disturbance by measurement.

3 · Linear algebra: vector spaces & operators notes 3 · Ch 13–14

  • Basis = linearly independent spanning list; $\dim V$ = length of any basis. $\mathcal L(V)$ = linear maps $V\to V$.
  • Matrix of $T$ in basis $\{v_j\}$: $Tv_j=\sum_iT_{ij}v_i$. Change of basis $A$: $T'=A^{-1}TA$; $\text{tr}$, $\det$ basis-independent.
  • $\text{null}\,T$, $\text{range}\,T$; rank–nullity: $\dim V=\dim\text{null}\,T+\dim\text{range}\,T$. Finite dim: injective ⇔ surjective ⇔ invertible ⇔ null $T=\{0\}$.
  • Eigenvalue $\lambda$: $T-\lambda\mathbb1$ not invertible ⇔ $\det(T-\lambda\mathbb1)=0$. Every operator on a finite-dim complex space has ≥1 eigenvalue. Eigenvectors with distinct eigenvalues are linearly independent.
  • Inner product: $\langle a,b\rangle$ linear in 2nd slot, $\langle a,b\rangle=\langle b,a\rangle^*$, $\langle a,a\rangle\ge0$ (=0 iff $a=0$).
$$\textbf{Cauchy–Schwarz: }|\langle u,v\rangle|\le|u|\,|v|,\qquad \textbf{Triangle: }|u+v|\le|u|+|v|$$ Gram–Schmidt: $e_1=\frac{v_1}{|v_1|}$, $e_j=\frac{v_j-\sum_{i<j}\langle e_i,v_j\rangle e_i}{|\cdots|}$.
Orthogonal projector onto $U$: $P_U v=\sum_i\langle e_i,v\rangle e_i$; $P_U^2=P_U$, $P_U^\dagger=P_U$, $V=U\oplus U^\perp$.
  • Riesz: every linear functional is $\phi(v)=\langle u,v\rangle$ for a unique $u$.
  • Adjoint: $\langle u,Tv\rangle=\langle T^\dagger u,v\rangle$; $(ST)^\dagger=T^\dagger S^\dagger$; $(T^\dagger)_{ij}=T^*_{ji}$ in an orthonormal basis.
  • Hermitian $T=T^\dagger$ ⇔ $\langle v,Tv\rangle\in\mathbb R$ ∀$v$ (complex space). Real eigenvalues; orthogonal eigenvectors.
  • Complex space: $\langle v,Tv\rangle=0$ ∀$v$ ⇒ $T=0$. (Fails for real spaces: rotations by 90°.)
  • Unitary $U^\dagger U=\mathbb1$ ⇔ preserves norms ⇔ maps orthonormal bases to orthonormal bases. Eigenvalues $|\lambda|=1$.
  • Functions of operators: $f(\hat A)=\sum_if(\lambda_i)P_i$ for diagonalizable $\hat A$; power series otherwise. $\frac{d}{dt}e^{t\hat A}=\hat Ae^{t\hat A}$; $e^{\hat A}e^{\hat B}=e^{\hat A+\hat B}$ only if $[\hat A,\hat B]=0$.

4 · Dirac notation & the axioms notes 4 · Ch 14, 16.6

$$\langle a|b\rangle=\langle a,b\rangle,\quad \langle a|b\rangle^*=\langle b|a\rangle,\quad (\alpha|a\rangle)^\dagger=\alpha^*\langle a|$$ $$\textbf{Completeness: }\sum_i|i\rangle\langle i|=\mathbb1,\qquad T=\sum_{i,j}|i\rangle\langle i|T|j\rangle\langle j|$$ $$\langle a|T^\dagger|b\rangle=\langle b|T|a\rangle^*,\qquad (|a\rangle\langle b|)^\dagger=|b\rangle\langle a|$$
  • Projector onto unit $|u\rangle$: $P=|u\rangle\langle u|$; onto subspace: $P=\sum_{i\in U}|i\rangle\langle i|$, $\text{tr}P=\dim U$.
  • Unitary between orthonormal bases: $U=\sum_i|f_i\rangle\langle e_i|$.
  • Insert $\mathbb1$ anywhere: $\langle a|AB|b\rangle=\sum_i\langle a|A|i\rangle\langle i|B|b\rangle$.
Continuous bases: $$\hat x|x\rangle=x|x\rangle,\quad\langle x|y\rangle=\delta(x-y),\quad \int\!dx\,|x\rangle\langle x|=\mathbb1,\quad \psi(x)=\langle x|\psi\rangle$$ $$\langle x|p\rangle=\frac{e^{ipx/\hbar}}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}},\quad \langle x|\hat p|\psi\rangle=\frac\hbar i\frac{d}{dx}\psi(x),\quad\langle p|\hat x|\psi\rangle=i\hbar\frac{d}{dp}\tilde\psi(p)$$ $$\langle\phi|\psi\rangle=\int\phi^*(x)\psi(x)dx=\int\tilde\phi^*(p)\tilde\psi(p)dp$$

$|x\rangle$, $|p\rangle$ are not normalizable (not in the Hilbert space) but form complete "bases".

Axioms (any isolated system): A1 a state is a ray in a Hilbert space ($|\Psi\rangle\sim c|\Psi\rangle$). A2 observables are Hermitian operators, $\hat A=\sum_ka_kP_k$ (spectral decomposition). A3 measurement along $\{P_k\}$ gives $k$ with $p(k)=\langle\Psi|P_k|\Psi\rangle$; the state becomes $P_k|\Psi\rangle/\sqrt{p(k)}$. A4 time evolution is unitary. Postulates: P1 composite system space = $\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B$. P2 identical particles: only totally symmetric (bosons) or antisymmetric (fermions) states. Open systems → density matrices (§11).

5 · Uncertainty & compatible observables notes 5 · Ch 15

$$\Delta A=\big|(\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle)\Psi\big|,\qquad (\Delta A)^2=\langle\hat A^2\rangle-\langle\hat A\rangle^2$$ $$\Delta A\,\Delta B\ \ge\ \Big|\Big\langle\Psi\Big|\frac{1}{2i}[\hat A,\hat B]\Big|\Psi\Big\rangle\Big|$$ Saturated iff $(\hat B-\langle\hat B\rangle)\Psi=i\lambda(\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle)\Psi$, $\lambda\in\mathbb R$. For $x,p$ ⇒ Gaussian $\psi\propto e^{-\lambda(x-\langle x\rangle)^2/2\hbar+i\langle p\rangle x/\hbar}$.

Proof sketch: $f=(\hat A-\langle A\rangle)\Psi$, $g=(\hat B-\langle B\rangle)\Psi$; Schwarz $|f||g|\ge|\langle f,g\rangle|\ge|\text{Im}\langle f,g\rangle|$.

Energy–time (Mandelstam–Tamm): for any $\hat Q$ (no explicit $t$), $$\Delta H\,\Delta Q\ge\frac\hbar2\Big|\frac{d\langle\hat Q\rangle}{dt}\Big|\ \Rightarrow\ \Delta H\,\Delta t\ge\frac\hbar2,\quad \Delta t\equiv\frac{\Delta Q}{|d\langle Q\rangle/dt|}$$ Decay: lifetime τ ↔ linewidth $\Delta E\sim\hbar/\tau$.
Lower bounds on $E_{\rm gs}$: write $\langle H\rangle$ in terms of $\langle p^2\rangle\ge(\Delta p)^2$, $\langle x^2\rangle\ge(\Delta x)^2$, $\langle x^4\rangle\ge\langle x^2\rangle^2$, then use $\Delta p\ge\hbar/2\Delta x$ and minimize. E.g. $H=\frac{p^2}{2m}+\alpha x^4$ ⇒ $E_{\rm gs}\ge\frac38\big(\frac{2\hbar^4\alpha}{m^2}\big)^{1/3}$. (Upper bound: variational.)
  • Diagonalizable ⇔ basis of eigenvectors; then $T=ADA^{-1}$. Unitarily diagonalizable ⇔ orthonormal eigenbasis.
  • Spectral theorem: $M$ unitarily diagonalizable ⇔ $M$ is normal ($[M,M^\dagger]=0$). Hermitian & unitary are normal. $M=\sum_k\lambda_kP_k$.
  • Simultaneous diagonalization: Hermitian $A,B$ have a common orthonormal eigenbasis ⇔ $[A,B]=0$ (compatible observables). Within each degenerate eigenspace of $A$, diagonalize $B$.
  • CSCO: set of commuting Hermitian operators whose eigenvalues uniquely label every basis state (e.g. $\{H,L^2,L_z\}$ for hydrogen without spin).

6 · Quantum dynamics notes 6 · Ch 16–17

Harmonic oscillator (algebraic)

$$\hat a=\sqrt{\tfrac{m\omega}{2\hbar}}\Big(\hat x+\tfrac{i\hat p}{m\omega}\Big),\ [\hat a,\hat a^\dagger]=1,\ \hat H=\hbar\omega\big(\hat N+\tfrac12\big),\ \hat N=\hat a^\dagger\hat a$$ $$\hat x=\sqrt{\tfrac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}(\hat a+\hat a^\dagger),\quad \hat p=i\sqrt{\tfrac{m\omega\hbar}{2}}(\hat a^\dagger-\hat a),\quad |n\rangle=\tfrac{(\hat a^\dagger)^n}{\sqrt{n!}}|0\rangle$$ $$\hat a|n\rangle=\sqrt n|n-1\rangle,\quad \hat a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle,\quad \langle x|0\rangle\propto e^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}$$

Schrödinger picture: unitary time evolution

$$|\Psi,t\rangle=\mathcal U(t,t_0)|\Psi,t_0\rangle,\quad \mathcal U^\dagger\mathcal U=\mathbb1,\quad \mathcal U(t_2,t_1)\mathcal U(t_1,t_0)=\mathcal U(t_2,t_0)$$ $$i\hbar\,\partial_t\,\mathcal U(t,t_0)=\hat H(t)\,\mathcal U(t,t_0)\quad\big(\hat H\equiv i\hbar(\partial_t\mathcal U)\mathcal U^\dagger\text{ is Hermitian}\big)$$ $$\textbf{(1) }\hat H\text{ const: }\ \mathcal U=e^{-i\hat H(t-t_0)/\hbar}$$ $$\textbf{(2) }[\hat H(t_1),\hat H(t_2)]=0:\ \ \mathcal U=\exp\Big[-\tfrac i\hbar\!\int_{t_0}^t\!\hat H(t')dt'\Big]$$ $$\textbf{(3) general: }\ \mathcal U=\mathcal T\exp\Big[-\tfrac i\hbar\!\int_{t_0}^t\!\hat H(t')dt'\Big]\ \text{(time-ordered, Dyson series)}$$

Heisenberg picture

$$\hat A_H(t)=\mathcal U^\dagger(t,0)\,\hat A_S\,\mathcal U(t,0),\qquad \langle\Psi,t|\hat A_S|\Psi,t\rangle=\langle\Psi,0|\hat A_H(t)|\Psi,0\rangle$$ $$i\hbar\frac{d\hat A_H}{dt}=[\hat A_H,\hat H_H]+i\hbar\Big(\frac{\partial\hat A_S}{\partial t}\Big)_H,\qquad [\hat A_H,\hat B_H]=[\hat A,\hat B]_H$$

$\hat H_H=\hat H_S$ if $\hat H_S$ is time-independent. Products: $(\hat A\hat B)_H=\hat A_H\hat B_H$.

Coherent states

$$\textbf{Translation: }\hat T_{x_0}=e^{-i\hat px_0/\hbar},\quad \hat T^\dagger_{x_0}\hat x\,\hat T_{x_0}=\hat x+x_0,\quad \langle x|\hat T_{x_0}|\psi\rangle=\psi(x-x_0)$$ $$|\alpha\rangle=\hat D(\alpha)|0\rangle=e^{\alpha\hat a^\dagger-\alpha^*\hat a}|0\rangle=e^{-|\alpha|^2/2}\sum_n\frac{\alpha^n}{\sqrt{n!}}|n\rangle$$ $$\hat a|\alpha\rangle=\alpha|\alpha\rangle,\qquad \alpha=\frac{\langle\hat x\rangle}{\sqrt2\,d}+i\frac{\langle\hat p\rangle\,d}{\sqrt2\,\hbar},\quad d=\sqrt{\tfrac{\hbar}{m\omega}}$$ $$|\alpha,t\rangle=e^{-i\omega t/2}\,|\alpha e^{-i\omega t}\rangle\quad\text{(stays coherent)}$$
  • $\langle x\rangle(t)$, $\langle p\rangle(t)$ follow the classical orbit; $\Delta x=\frac{d}{\sqrt2}$, $\Delta p=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt2 d}$ (vacuum values, minimum uncertainty) for all $t$.
  • Number distribution is Poisson: $P(n)=e^{-|\alpha|^2}\frac{|\alpha|^{2n}}{n!}$, $\langle N\rangle=|\alpha|^2$, $\Delta N=|\alpha|$.
  • Not orthogonal: $|\langle\beta|\alpha\rangle|^2=e^{-|\alpha-\beta|^2}$; overcomplete. $\hat D^\dagger(\alpha)\hat a\hat D(\alpha)=\hat a+\alpha$.
  • Real $\alpha$: $\hat D(\alpha)=\hat T_{x_0}$ with $x_0=\sqrt2\,d\,\alpha$.
BCH: if $[\hat A,\hat B]$ commutes with $\hat A$ and $\hat B$: $e^{\hat A+\hat B}=e^{\hat A}e^{\hat B}e^{-\frac12[\hat A,\hat B]}$. $$e^{\hat A}\hat Be^{-\hat A}=\hat B+[\hat A,\hat B]+\tfrac1{2!}[\hat A,[\hat A,\hat B]]+\cdots$$

Squeezed states

$$\hat S(\gamma)=\exp\!\Big[-\frac\gamma2\big(\hat a^\dagger\hat a^\dagger-\hat a\hat a\big)\Big],\quad |0_\gamma\rangle=\hat S(\gamma)|0\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\cosh\gamma}}e^{-\frac12\tanh\gamma\,\hat a^\dagger\hat a^\dagger}|0\rangle$$ $$\Delta x=e^{-\gamma}\sqrt{\tfrac{\hbar}{2m\omega}},\qquad \Delta p=e^{\gamma}\sqrt{\tfrac{m\omega\hbar}{2}},\qquad \Delta x\Delta p=\tfrac\hbar2$$ $(\hat a\cosh\gamma+\hat a^\dagger\sinh\gamma)|0_\gamma\rangle=0$. Contains only even $n$. General: $\hat D(\alpha)\hat S(\gamma)|0\rangle$.

Origin: ground state of an oscillator with frequency $\omega_1$, written in terms of a different oscillator $\omega_2$; $e^{\gamma}=\sqrt{\omega_1/\omega_2}$. Uncertainty ellipse rotates at $2\omega$ under time evolution.

Phase space ($\langle x\rangle/d$, $\langle p\rangle d/\hbar$): coherent state = vacuum disc carried around a classical circle; squeezed state = ellipse with the same area.

Photon states: each EM mode ↔ oscillator; $\hat E\propto(\hat a e^{-i\omega t}+\hat a^\dagger e^{i\omega t})$; $|n\rangle$ = $n$ photons, $\langle\hat E\rangle=0$; coherent state = classical wave; squeezed light reduces noise in one quadrature (LIGO).

Factorization method (Ch 17.8). Write $\hat H=\hat A^\dagger\hat A+E_0$ with $\hat A=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2m}}\big(\frac{d}{dx}+W(x)\big)$, so $V=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}(W^2-W')+E_0$. Then $\hat A\psi_0=0$ gives the ground state $\psi_0\propto e^{-\int W}$, energy $E_0$. The partner $\hat H'=\hat A\hat A^\dagger+E_0$ has the same spectrum minus $E_0$, and $\hat A$ maps eigenstates of $\hat H$ to those of $\hat H'$. SHO: $W=\frac{m\omega}{\hbar}x$. Works for the hydrogen radial equation too.

7 · Two-state systems notes 7 · Ch 17

$$\vec\mu=\gamma\hat{\vec S},\quad\gamma=\frac{gq}{2m},\quad \hat H=-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B=-\gamma\vec B\cdot\hat{\vec S}$$ Electron: $g\approx2$, $\vec\mu=-\frac{e}{m}\hat{\vec S}$, $\mu_B=\frac{e\hbar}{2m}$. $$\textbf{Larmor: }\ \hat H=\vec\omega_L\cdot\hat{\vec S},\quad \vec\omega_L=-\gamma\vec B,\qquad \frac{d\langle\hat{\vec S}\rangle}{dt}=\vec\omega_L\times\langle\hat{\vec S}\rangle$$ $$\hat R_{\hat n}(\alpha)=e^{-i\alpha\hat n\cdot\hat{\vec S}/\hbar}\ \text{rotates spin states by }\alpha\text{ about }\hat n$$ $\mathcal U(t)=\hat R_{\hat\omega_L}(\omega_Lt)$. Note $\hat R_{\hat n}(2\pi)=-\mathbb1$ for spin ½.
Any two-state Hamiltonian is a spin in a field: $$\hat H=g_0\mathbb1+\vec g\cdot\vec\sigma=g_0\mathbb1+\frac{2}{\hbar}\vec g\cdot\hat{\vec S}\ \Rightarrow\ \vec\omega=\frac{2\vec g}{\hbar}$$ Energies $g_0\pm|\vec g|$, eigenstates $|n;\pm\rangle$ with $\hat n=\vec g/|\vec g|$. Map state $\alpha|1\rangle+\beta|2\rangle$ to a Bloch vector and precess it about $\vec\omega$.

Ammonia molecule

Basis $|\!\uparrow\rangle,|\!\downarrow\rangle$ (N above/below H plane); tunneling couples them:

$$\hat H=\begin{pmatrix}E_0&-\Delta\\-\Delta&E_0\end{pmatrix}$$$$|G\rangle=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|\!\uparrow\rangle+|\!\downarrow\rangle),\ E_0-\Delta;\qquad |E\rangle=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|\!\uparrow\rangle-|\!\downarrow\rangle),\ E_0+\Delta$$ $2\Delta\approx0.987\times10^{-4}$ eV, $\nu=23.87$ GHz (λ ≈ 1.26 cm). Starting in $|\!\uparrow\rangle$: oscillates to $|\!\downarrow\rangle$ at frequency $2\Delta/\hbar$.
In a static field $\mathcal E$ (dipole $\mu$): $\hat H=\begin{pmatrix}E_0+\mu\mathcal E&-\Delta\\-\Delta&E_0-\mu\mathcal E\end{pmatrix}$, $E_\pm=E_0\pm\sqrt{\Delta^2+\mu^2\mathcal E^2}$. Field gradient separates $|E\rangle$, $|G\rangle$ beams.
Maser: $\mathcal E(t)=2\mathcal E_0\cos\omega_0t$, $\hbar\omega_0=2\Delta$; drop $e^{\pm2i\omega_0t}$ terms (rotating-wave approx.) ⇒ $P_{E\to G}(t)=\sin^2\big(\frac{\mu\mathcal E_0t}{\hbar}\big)$.

Nuclear magnetic resonance

$$\vec B(t)=B_0\hat z+B_1(\hat x\cos\omega t-\hat y\sin\omega t),\qquad \omega_0=\gamma B_0,\ \ \omega_1=\gamma B_1$$ Rotating frame $|\Psi_R\rangle=e^{-i\omega t\hat S_z/\hbar}|\Psi\rangle$ gives a time-independent field $$\vec B_R=B_1\hat x+B_0\Big(1-\frac{\omega}{\omega_0}\Big)\hat z,\qquad |\Psi,t\rangle=e^{i\omega t\hat S_z/\hbar}e^{i\gamma\vec B_R\cdot\hat{\vec S}t/\hbar}|\Psi,0\rangle$$ Resonance $\omega=\omega_0$: $\vec B_R=B_1\hat x$, spin rotates slowly ($\omega_1$) from $z$ toward the plane while precessing fast ($\omega_0$). Spin-flip prob. (Rabi): $P=\frac{\omega_1^2}{\Omega^2}\sin^2\frac{\Omega t}{2}$, $\Omega=\sqrt{(\omega-\omega_0)^2+\omega_1^2}$.

90° pulse ($\omega_1t=\pi/2$) tips spins into $xy$-plane; precessing magnetization induces signal. Relaxation $T_1$ (longitudinal), $T_2$ (transverse) give MRI contrast. Proton: $\gamma/2\pi\approx42.6$ MHz/T.

Rabi oscillations of spin-flip probability: on resonance (blue) full flips; detuned $|\omega-\omega_0|=\omega_1$ (red) max ½, faster.

8 · Tensor products & entanglement notes 8 · Ch 18

  • $V\otimes W$: spanned by $v\otimes w$; $\dim=\dim V\cdot\dim W$. Bilinear: $(a v_1+bv_2)\otimes w=a\,v_1\otimes w+b\,v_2\otimes w$.
  • $(\hat S\otimes\hat T)(v\otimes w)=\hat Sv\otimes\hat Tw$; $\hat S\otimes\mathbb1$ and $\mathbb1\otimes\hat T$ commute. $\langle v_1\otimes w_1|v_2\otimes w_2\rangle=\langle v_1|v_2\rangle\langle w_1|w_2\rangle$.
  • Entangled = not a product $v\otimes w$. Two qubits $a|{+}{+}\rangle+b|{+}{-}\rangle+c|{-}{+}\rangle+d|{-}{-}\rangle$ is a product state ⇔ $ad-bc=0$.
  • Measuring particle 1 of $\sum_i|i\rangle_1\otimes|\phi_i\rangle_2$ with result $i$ leaves 2 in $|\phi_i\rangle$ (normalized); prob $=\langle\phi_i|\phi_i\rangle$.
Bell basis $|\Phi_i\rangle=(\mathbb1\otimes\sigma_i)|\Phi_0\rangle$, orthonormal, all maximally entangled: $$|\Phi_0\rangle=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|{+}{+}\rangle+|{-}{-}\rangle),\quad |\Phi_1\rangle=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|{+}{-}\rangle+|{-}{+}\rangle)$$ $$|\Phi_2\rangle=\tfrac{i}{\sqrt2}(|{+}{-}\rangle-|{-}{+}\rangle),\quad |\Phi_3\rangle=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|{+}{+}\rangle-|{-}{-}\rangle)$$ $$|{+}{+}\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(|\Phi_0\rangle+|\Phi_3\rangle),\quad |{-}{-}\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(|\Phi_0\rangle-|\Phi_3\rangle)$$ $$|{+}{-}\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(|\Phi_1\rangle-i|\Phi_2\rangle),\quad |{-}{+}\rangle=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(|\Phi_1\rangle+i|\Phi_2\rangle)$$
Teleportation. Alice holds unknown $|\Psi\rangle_C=\alpha|+\rangle+\beta|-\rangle$ and $A$ of a shared $|\Phi_0\rangle_{AB}$. Rewriting, $$|\Psi\rangle_C\otimes|\Phi_0\rangle_{AB}=\tfrac12\sum_{i=0}^{3}|\Phi_i\rangle_{CA}\otimes\sigma_i|\Psi\rangle_B\quad(\text{up to phases})$$ Alice does a Bell measurement on $CA$ (result $i$, 2 classical bits) → Bob applies $\sigma_i$ → $B$ is in $|\Psi\rangle$. Original destroyed (no-cloning); no FTL signalling.
EPR & Bell. Singlet $|\psi\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}(|{+}{-}\rangle-|{-}{+}\rangle)$ (rotation invariant): $$\langle(\vec a\cdot\vec\sigma)\otimes(\vec b\cdot\vec\sigma)\rangle=-\vec a\cdot\vec b,\qquad P(\vec a{+},\vec b{+})=\tfrac12\sin^2\tfrac{\theta_{ab}}{2}$$ Local hidden variables ⇒ Bell inequality $P(a,b)\le P(a,c)+P(c,b)$. QM with coplanar $a,c,b$ at $\theta,\theta$: $\sin^2\theta\le2\sin^2\frac\theta2$ — violated for small θ. Experiments side with QM.
  • Traces: $\text{tr}(\hat A\otimes\hat B)=\text{tr}\hat A\,\text{tr}\hat B$. Partial trace $\text{tr}_B\big(\hat A\otimes\hat B\big)=\hat A\,\text{tr}\hat B$, extended linearly.
  • No-cloning: no unitary $\mathcal U|\psi\rangle|e\rangle=|\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle$ for all $|\psi\rangle$. Unitarity preserves $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle$, but cloning would need $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle=\langle\phi|\psi\rangle^2$, true only for identical or orthogonal states.

9 · Angular momentum notes 9 · Ch 19

11 · Density matrix & decoherence Ch 22

Why: a state vector can't describe a subsystem of an entangled pair, or a classical mixture from an imperfect source. The density matrix handles both.

$$\hat\rho=\sum_ip_i|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|,\qquad \hat\rho^\dagger=\hat\rho,\quad \hat\rho\ge0,\quad \text{tr}\,\hat\rho=1$$ $$\langle\hat A\rangle=\text{tr}(\hat\rho\hat A),\qquad p(k)=\text{tr}(P_k\hat\rho),\qquad i\hbar\frac{d\hat\rho}{dt}=[\hat H,\hat\rho]$$ Pure ⇔ $\hat\rho^2=\hat\rho$ ⇔ $\text{tr}\,\hat\rho^2=1$; mixed: $\text{tr}\,\hat\rho^2<1$. Different ensembles can give the same $\hat\rho$, and then no measurement can tell them apart.
Qubit: $\hat\rho=\frac12(\mathbb1+\vec a\cdot\vec\sigma)$, $|\vec a|\le1$: surface of the Bloch ball = pure, interior = mixed, centre = maximally mixed.
Subsystems: $\hat\rho_A=\text{tr}_B\hat\rho_{AB}$ gives every prediction for measurements on $A$ alone.
Schmidt decomposition: any $|\psi\rangle_{AB}=\sum_i\sqrt{p_i}\,|a_i\rangle|b_i\rangle$ (orthonormal sets). $\hat\rho_A$ and $\hat\rho_B$ share eigenvalues $p_i$. Entangled ⇔ Schmidt rank > 1. Entanglement entropy $S=-\sum_ip_i\ln p_i$ (singlet: $\ln2$, maximal).
Open systems & decoherence: system $S$ becomes entangled with environment $E$, so $\hat\rho_S$ loses its off-diagonal terms in a preferred (pointer) basis: superposition → effectively classical mixture. Markovian evolution is the Lindblad equation $$\frac{d\hat\rho}{dt}=-\frac i\hbar[\hat H,\hat\rho]+\sum_k\Big(\hat L_k\hat\rho\hat L_k^\dagger-\tfrac12\{\hat L_k^\dagger\hat L_k,\hat\rho\}\Big)$$ e.g. dephasing $\hat L=\sqrt{\gamma}\,\sigma_z$ shrinks the off-diagonal $\rho_{01}$ by $e^{-2\gamma t}$; decay $\hat L=\sqrt{\Gamma}\,\sigma_-$ relaxes to $|0\rangle$.

Measurement problem: decoherence explains why we never see superpositions of pointer states, but not why one particular outcome occurs. Interpretations (Copenhagen, many-worlds, …) differ on that point.

12 · Quantum computation Ch 23

Qubits & gates: $|0\rangle,|1\rangle$; $n$ qubits span $2^n$ dimensions. Gates are unitaries: $$X=\sigma_1,\quad Z=\sigma_3,\quad H=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad \text{CNOT}|x\rangle|y\rangle=|x\rangle|y\oplus x\rangle$$ $H|0\rangle=|+\rangle$, $H|1\rangle=|-\rangle$; $H^2=\mathbb1$; $HXH=Z$. H then CNOT on $|00\rangle$ makes the Bell state $|\Phi_0\rangle$.
Oracle: $U_f|x\rangle|y\rangle=|x\rangle|y\oplus f(x)\rangle$. With the target in $|-\rangle$: $U_f|x\rangle|-\rangle=(-1)^{f(x)}|x\rangle|-\rangle$ (phase kickback).
Deutsch: $f:\{0,1\}\to\{0,1\}$. Prepare $|+\rangle|-\rangle$, apply $U_f$, then $H$ on qubit 1: it reads $0$ if $f$ is constant and $1$ if balanced. One query instead of two: interference extracts a global property.
Grover search ($N$ items, one marked $|w\rangle$, uniform start $|s\rangle$): repeat oracle $\mathbb1-2|w\rangle\langle w|$ then diffusion $2|s\rangle\langle s|-\mathbb1$. Each round rotates the state by $2\theta$ toward $|w\rangle$ in the $(|w\rangle,|s\rangle)$ plane, $\sin\theta=1/\sqrt N$: $$P_{\rm success}(k)=\sin^2\big((2k+1)\theta\big),$$$$k_{\rm opt}\simeq\frac\pi4\sqrt N\quad(\text{vs }\sim N/2\text{ classically})$$ Overshooting lowers the success probability.

Where Are the Superintelligences?

Prashanth Shankar · November 2026

Also as a video: The Fermi Paradox of Superintelligence

Superintelligence is a system or a being that has figured out recursive self-improvement. Such a thing can learn, create knowledge, and improve itself. Humans are a clear example of beings that can do that. We are intelligent. What makes something superintelligent is the speed at which it can do this.

If something can learn and create knowledge at or near the speed of light, it can make progress in every possible field. It can discover the laws of nature, use them to build things, and use those things to speed up its own progress, getting ever faster.

Before we know it, it is so superior in intelligence, and its knowledge of the universe is so far ahead of ours, that many believe we will never be able to understand it. Its knowledge and ability to do things are so far beyond ours that people think not only will we be unable to keep up, but we will not even know what such a thing can do or will look like. As they say, we would be to a superintelligence what ants are to us. A being with supernatural powers by any measure.

If such a thing is possible, then in this vast universe, it is not implausible to think that it could emerge not only on Earth but also in many other places, just as we think life elsewhere could exist in different forms.

What if such a thing exists somewhere? What would it look like? Why have we not seen it?

Unlike aliens, which could exist without making too much cosmic noise, a superintelligence could be harnessing energy from stars and turning galaxies into coffee machines. Its signatures would be all over the universe.

Yet why have we not seen any of them?

There could be a few reasons:

  1. Such a thing has not emerged yet. It may still be in its infancy, and no fully developed superintelligence exists anywhere in the universe, including on Earth.
  2. Superintelligences exist, but they have not reached us yet. They may be turning galaxies into coffee machines, but they are so far away that, in our observations, those galaxies are still galaxies. The light carrying evidence of their activities has not reached us yet.
  3. Superintelligences exist, but all they want to do is sit and meditate. In which case, there would be no observable astronomical signatures of their existence, despite their extraordinary capabilities.
  4. They exist and have reached us. If they are here, there are a few possibilities.
    • What if they're bad guys? In which case, they would have destroyed us already.
    • What if they're good guys? Why have they not helped us cure cancer?
    • Or what if they have decided not to intervene? Imagine someone discovering a cure for cancer in the UK and never telling anyone in America because they don't want to intervene. That would be evil too.
  5. They are here and are interacting with us, but we don't know it. But that is the same argument one could make about ghosts.
  6. Whatever the case, we humans simply cannot know. But that is the same argument humans once made about stars and comets.

So, what is going on? Where are the superintelligences?
